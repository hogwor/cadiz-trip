Tue 24 NovRain day

Rest & Indoors

The only rain day forecast · no car needed

  • Sleep in. Recover from the travel day.
  • Getting there: Line 1 bus, one block inland on Av. de Andalucía, every ~6 min to Plaza de España (end of line), then ~10 min walk. Back: Line 1 toward Cortadura. Fare €1.10 cash per adult; under-15s free (ask the driver).
  • Mercado Central de Abastos — covered market, lunch at the stalls. Winds down ~14:00–15:00.
  • Shopping: Plaza de las Flores next to the market, then Calle Columela, San Francisco, Ancha. Small shops close ~14:00–17:00; chains stay open.
  • Museo de Cádiz — Phoenician sarcophagi, small, an hour tops.
  • Cathedral interior + crypt — save the tower for Sunday when there's a view.

Christmas in Cádiz

Cádiz switches on its Christmas lights the last weekend of November. It was Sat 30 Nov in 2024 and Fri 28 Nov in 2025, both 19:00 in Plaza de San Juan de Dios. For 2026 that means Fri 27 or Sat 28 — inside your week.

Saturday evening is the safe bet. If the switch-on is Friday, the lights are already up. If it's Saturday, you walk into the inauguration itself. Friday is the one night to skip — the session runs from 15:00 and a 19:00 ceremony likely collides.

Better than the market

Zambombas in Jerez

Flamenco Christmas singalongs in bodegas and plazas — declared Bien de Interés Cultural and a Fiesta de Interés Turístico. Mostly free.

In 2025 they ran 14 Nov – 24 Dec, intensifying from 21 Nov, which puts your entire week in peak season. Jerez is Thursday's horse-show-and-bodega trip — pair a daytime tour with an evening zambomba and you've got the real Andalusian Christmas.

Most cluster on weekends, so a Thursday one is less certain. The agenda usually publishes in early-to-mid November.

Why Thursday ends early

Friday's session runs into the evening and Saturday is an 08:30 pool call. Get back from Jerez Thursday with time for a normal dinner and a real night's sleep.

Night of 29 November

Málaga Airport — To Book

All four have free parking, which matters more than a shuttle when you've got a rental to return.

Parador de Málaga Golf

Best last night

Churriana, ~10 min to the terminal. State-run parador, 4-star, on the golf course near Los Álamos beach. Actual restaurant, actual grounds — the one that feels like a reward after two days on a pool deck rather than a bed by a runway.

4-starFree parking~10 min to AGPRestaurant on site

Holiday Inn Express Málaga Airport

Closest

Churriana, ~1 km from the terminal — the nearest hotel to AGP. Family rooms take two adults plus two kids on a sofa bed, under-17s free in the parents' room. 24-hour desk, very early breakfast. Reviews are mixed on cleanliness and service; sources disagree on whether the shuttle is complimentary, so confirm if it matters.

Torremolinos, quiet residential area. Free airport shuttle and a heated outdoor pool — the shuttle is moot with a rental car, but confirm shuttle times with reception if you'd rather drop the car the night before.
