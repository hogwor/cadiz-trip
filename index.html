The one real decision

Drive to Málaga on Sunday evening instead of 05:00 Monday. The meet ends Sunday afternoon; a 3-hour drive that evening turns a pre-dawn scramble into a normal morning. See Stay.

Mon 23 NovTravel

Land, Rota, Cádiz

Málaga → NAS Rota → Cádiz · ~3h20 driving + stop

  • Land 09:30. Intra-Schengen, so bags only — no passport control.
  • NAS Rota commissary — snacks and groceries for the picky eater.
  • Into Cádiz ~15:00–15:30. Check in, walk Playa Victoria.
  • Dinner: tapas near Plaza de la Catedral.
Tue 24 NovDay trip

Jerez de la Frontera

~35 min each way

  • Sherry bodega tour — Tío Pepe / González Byass. Both walk the cellars, works fine with kids.
  • Royal Andalusian School of Equestrian Art — shows run select days only. Check the schedule and build the day around it.
Wed 25 NovDay trip

Vejer & Cabo de Trafalgar

~45–60 min south

  • Vejer de la Frontera — hilltop pueblo blanco, one of the prettiest in Spain. Lunch here.
  • Cabo de Trafalgar — lighthouse and a wide empty beach. Windy November walk, not a swim.
Thu 26 NovLight day

Cádiz Old Town

No car — the old town is walkable and dense

  • Torre Tavira — camera obscura, best rooftop view in the city.
  • Cathedral — go up the tower, not just inside.
  • Mercado Central de Abastos for lunch; wander Barrio del Pópulo.
  • Parque Genovés. Early night — meet starts tomorrow.
Fri 27 NovMeet · evening

Ferry Day, Then Racing

Vaporcito ferry · ~30–40 min each way · no driving

  • Morning: ferry across the bay to El Puerto de Santa María. Wander, lunch on the water.
  • Back to Cádiz mid-afternoon. Rest before the session — deliberately no driving today.
  • Evening: first meet session.
Sat 28 NovMeet

Full Meet Day

  • Sessions eat the day. Gaps are for tapas near the venue, not excursions.
  • Walk to the pool — 10–15 min from the flat. Don't move the car.
Sun 29 NovMeet + drive

Finals, Then Head East

Cádiz → Málaga ~2h40–3h (if taking the Sunday-night option)

  • Check out of the Cádiz flat in the morning and load the car before the session.
  • Meet finishes; drive to a hotel near Málaga airport, arrive ~20:00.
  • Real dinner, real sleep. See Stay for the picks.

Why Friday is a ferry, not a road trip

The girls swim Friday evening. Stacking a driving day in front of that costs them the session. The ferry gets you out of the flat without putting anyone in a car seat for two hours.

Night of 29 November

Málaga Airport — To Book

All four have free parking, which matters more than a shuttle when you've got a rental to return.

Parador de Málaga Golf

Best last night

Churriana, ~10 min to the terminal. State-run parador, 4-star, on the golf course near Los Álamos beach. Actual restaurant, actual grounds — the one that feels like a reward after three days on a pool deck rather than a bed by a runway.

4-starFree parking~10 min to AGPRestaurant on site

Holiday Inn Express Málaga Airport

Closest

Churriana, ~1 km from the terminal — the nearest hotel to AGP. Family rooms take two adults plus two kids on a sofa bed, under-17s free in the parents' room. 24-hour desk, very early breakfast. Reviews are mixed on cleanliness and service; sources disagree on whether the shuttle is complimentary, so confirm if it matters.

Free parking~1 km to AGPFamily rooms24h deskMixed reviews

Hotel Zen Airport

Has a pool

Torremolinos, quiet residential area. Free airport shuttle and a heated outdoor pool — the shuttle is moot with a rental car, but confirm shuttle times with reception if you'd rather drop the car the night before.

Free shuttleHeated poolTorremolinos

Travelodge Málaga Airport

Cheapest

3-star, 114 rooms, free on-site parking. No frills — a clean bed near the airport and nothing more.

Trade-off to weigh

Option B costs one hotel night and means checking out of Cádiz on Sunday morning — bags in the car through the final session. What it buys is a 07:00 wake-up instead of 04:30 and a flight you won't miss.
